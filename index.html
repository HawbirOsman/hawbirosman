I'm a 26-year-old Network and Systems Administrator based in Sulaymaniyah, Iraq, currently managing enterprise IT infrastructure for Hiwa Rauf for Investment and Development. My work spans the full stack of corporate IT — identity, devices, email, networking, security, and backup.

I came up through IT help desk roles before moving into infrastructure ownership, which means I still think about the end user even while configuring firewall ACLs or Entra ID conditional access policies. That same instinct led me to start building internal software — including a Suggestion and Complaint Portal that gave the HR department a tool that didn't exist before.

I hold a B.Sc. in Information Technology from the American University of Iraq, Sulaymaniyah, with a minor in Business Management, and I'm certified in Fortinet's FCF and FCA cybersecurity tracks.
